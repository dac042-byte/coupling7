Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2025

Welcome to Coupling! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Name and email address
  • University and country information
  • User type (technical or non-technical)
  • Profile information including bio, skills, or project ideas
  • Previous projects or project ideas you choose to share

Usage Information

We automatically collect certain information when you use Coupling:

  • Swipe history and matching preferences
  • Messages sent through our platform
  • Usage patterns and interaction with features
  • Device information and IP address

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our matching service
  • Match you with compatible users (technical with non-technical)
  • Facilitate communication between matched users
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Send you important updates about your account
  • Ensure platform security and prevent fraud
  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information:

  • With Other Users: Your profile information is visible to potential matches
  • With Your Consent: We may share information when you give us permission
  • For Legal Reasons: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, sale, or acquisition

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Security measures include:

  • Password encryption using industry-standard hashing (bcrypt)
  • Secure HTTP connections (HTTPS)
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal data by our team

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@coupling.app (this is a placeholder - actual service would require a real email).

6. Student Privacy

Coupling is designed exclusively for college (and possibly high school) students. We are committed to protecting student privacy:

  • We verify university email addresses where possible
  • We do not share student data with educational institutions without consent
  • We comply with applicable student privacy laws (FERPA, COPPA where applicable)
  • Users under 18 require parental consent (if we expand to high school students)

7. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain your session and improve your experience. We use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for authentication and security
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings (like theme preference)
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our platform

8. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics, hosting, and other purposes. These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

9. International Users

Coupling is based in the United States. If you access our service from outside the US, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US. By using Coupling, you consent to this transfer.

10. Children's Privacy

Coupling is intended for users 18 and older (college students). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete it promptly.

If we expand to high school students (16+), we will implement appropriate parental consent mechanisms and additional privacy protections.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by:

  • Posting the new policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending an email notification for material changes

12. California Privacy Rights

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information
  • Right to deletion of personal information
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights

Note: We do not sell personal information.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@coupling.app (placeholder)
  • Feedback Form: Available in the app under Profile > Send Feedback

14. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile will be immediately removed from matching
  • Personal data will be deleted within 30 days
  • Some data may be retained for legal compliance (e.g., transaction records)
  • Anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics

15. Consent

By using Coupling, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our service.
